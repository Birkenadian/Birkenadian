# Project: Book Two Pitch for Dennis at Quarto

## Goal

Get Dennis at Quarto a document of developed concepts for Andrew's second book, soon, while their "any ideas?" exchange is fresh. Dennis is managing publication of *Zen and the Art of Record Collecting* (Quarto, early 2027). Andrew asked Dennis for ideas; Dennis said he would think on it and asked Andrew the same.

## Deliverables in this folder

- `spec.md` is the contract for the build. The /spec, /build, and /review skills work against it.
- `book2-pitches.md` is the pitch document, five ranked concepts. `book2-pitches.docx` is the formatted Word version for attaching to the email.
- `cover-email-dennis.md` is the short email that carries the attachment.
- `needle-drop-expanded.md` and `mastering-engineers-expanded.md` are fuller treatments of concepts 1 and 2.
- `review.md`, when present, is the open fix list from the last failed review.
- `memory.md` is the running log. Read it at the start of every session on this project.

## Standing decisions

All concepts stay in Andrew's lane: record collecting, music, listening culture, mindfulness, meditation. All writing follows the style rules in the root `CLAUDE.md` without exception. Dennis is addressed by first name only; his surname is unknown. Do not commit Andrew to dates in anything addressed to Dennis.
